H

Open source browser extension

Fast tab switching for Helium

A compact MRU popup for people who keep too many tabs open. Press a shortcut, tap Q to move, release the modifier to switch, or search by title and URL.

Features

Built around muscle memory.

MRU first

Recently used tabs stay at the top, even after the MV3 service worker restarts.

Release to switch

Use the popup like an app switcher: hold the modifier, cycle, release.

Fast search

Type parts of a title or URL. Multi-token fuzzy matching ranks the right tab higher.

Small controls

Choose popup size, current-window-only mode, pinned tab visibility, and search restore.

Shortcuts

Two popups, one workflow.

Open alt-tab-style popup ⌥Q
Search in alt-tab-style popup ⌥W
Move down Q
Move up ⇧Q

Open source

Apache-2.0 and built in public.
